## 🙋‍♂️ About me

I'm a full-stack developer and founder with 15+ years in technology. I like taking a product from a blank page to production: the mobile app, the backend, the database, and the servers it all runs on.

Most of my work is practical software for real businesses. I care about apps that are fast, simple to use, and keep working when the internet doesn't.

- 🔭 **Working on:** offline-first mobile apps and multi-tenant SaaS platforms
- 🧠 **Exploring:** AI agents, chatbots, and Hindi/Hinglish voice assistants
- ⚙️ **Deep into:** data sync, conflict resolution, and system design
- 🤝 **Open to:** collaborations on Flutter, Laravel and AI projects
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Flutter, Laravel, SaaS architecture, billing and accounting systems
